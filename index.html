How it works

Three steps, and then it's out of your hands.

  1. Step 01

    Pick your days

    Seven days, thirty, ninety — you choose at the end, not the start. The clock begins the moment the device actually locks, so a setup you abandon on Tuesday doesn't quietly eat Wednesday.

  2. Step 02

    We generate a passcode you never see

    The extension makes a random Screen Time passcode, encrypts it, and backs it up before showing you anything. Then it plays the digits one keypad tap at a time, over thirty seconds.

  3. Step 03

    Type it in. Walk away.

    Restrictions go on and the passcode is gone from your screen and your head. Come back when the days are up and unlock it from your device list.

Nothing you'd have to trust us about.

Common questions

Before you install it.

What happens if I lose the passcode?

You never had it. It's generated, encrypted, and backed up to your account before it's shown, and it's only ever shown as one keypad tap at a time. Recovering it is a normal, expected thing to do — not an emergency.

Does this work on Android or Windows?

Not today. AdultXBlocker walks you through iOS Screen Time, so the device being locked is an iPhone or iPad. The extension itself runs in Chrome on any desktop.

What if I close the popup halfway through setup?

Chrome destroys extension popups whenever they lose focus, so the setup is cached and picks up where it left off. A device left mid-setup shows as Pending in your list and can be resumed days later.

Do I need an account?

Yes — a verified email and password. That account is what the encrypted passcode backup belongs to, and it's what lets you see your devices from a different computer.

What data does the extension collect?

One Chrome permission: storage. It never asks for your tabs, your browsing history, Google Drive, or access to your files. Read the full docs.
